About Kaliganj Hands

Together We Build, Together We Grow

Our Story

Started in 2020 by local friends during the pandemic. Today, we are hundreds of hands working together for Kaliganj.

Our Purpose

Bridging the gap between those who want to help and those who need it.

Our Vision

A self-reliant Kaliganj where no child sleeps hungry and every elderly feels cared for.

Winter Relief

Winter Distribution

Our Winter Distribution program helps underprivileged মানুষদের শীতের কষ্ট থেকে রক্ষা করতে। We provide blankets, warm clothes, and ভালোবাসা to those who need it most. Our volunteers go door-to-door in remote villages.
